Voice & greeting
Jarvis’s brain is Nova
  • Say “Hey Jarvis” and your question in one go (“Hey Jarvis, what is 25% of 200?”), or say “Jarvis”, wait for the greeting, then ask. For about 8 seconds after he answers you can ask again without saying “Jarvis”.
  • Jarvis has no knowledge of his own. Every question goes to Nova, so he only knows what Nova knows (math, word problems, its lessons) and what you teach it. When Nova doesn’t know, he says “I do not know” honestly instead of guessing.
  • Teach by voice: when he says he doesn’t know, or asks “what do you mean by it?” or “yes or no?”, just say the answer within about 15 seconds, no “Jarvis” needed. He’ll reply “got it. next time I’ll say …”. Say “skip” or “never mind” to cancel.
  • Nova’s commands work by voice too: “when I say hello say welcome back”, “lock photo as a picture”, “what do you remember”, “start learning”, “stop learning”.
  • “Open Spotify / YouTube / Chrome” lights up that button. iPhone needs your tap to open it.
  • Memory on iPhone: in Safari, Jarvis and Nova share one memory, so things taught in one are known in the other. Each Home Screen app gets its own separate storage, so the Jarvis icon and the Nova icon won’t share what you teach. Both still start with the same Nova brain. Jarvis needs the internet the first time to load Nova.
Voice engine & debug 

      
      
      
On-device speech runs a small Whisper model on your phone. It is downloaded once from this site and kept for offline use. Your voice never leaves the phone.

    


    

      “Jarvis” voice wake
      
      

      
    
        
  • Jarvis only listens while this app is open on screen. iPhone web apps can’t listen in the background or with the screen locked.
    • 
        
  • The first time, iOS may ask for Speech Recognition permission as well as the microphone. Allow both.
    • 
        
  • Apple’s speech recognition in web apps is limited and can stop or miss words. Jarvis restarts it automatically. If voice wake doesn’t work from the Home Screen icon, try opening the link straight in Safari instead.
    • 
        
  • If voice wake and clap detection fight over the mic on your phone, voice wake takes priority. Clapping is the backup that always works when voice isn’t available.
    • 
        
  • Jarvis stops listening while it talks so it doesn’t hear itself.
